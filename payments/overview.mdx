Accept payments from your customers in one integration. Fincra handles the payment method, credits your wallet and tells you when each payment succeeds.

## Ways to accept payments

- **Checkout**: send your customer to a Fincra-hosted payment page. It shows the payment methods available to them.
- **Payment links**: create a link and share it by email, chat or social media. No code needed on your side.
- **Direct charges**: charge a card, bank transfer or mobile money wallet from your own payment page.

## After a payment

Every payment becomes a collection, Fincra's record of money you received. Use it to confirm the payment, issue a refund or respond to a chargeback.
