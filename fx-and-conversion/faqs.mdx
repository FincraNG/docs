## Rates and quotes

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a quote to convert?">
    Yes. Every conversion needs a `quoteReference`. Create a quote, then create the conversion with it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How long is a quote valid?">
    30 seconds. If it expires, create a new one. In the dashboard, a new quote is created for you automatically.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I use one quote twice?">
    No. Each quote can be used for one conversion.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What's the difference between a rate and a quote?">
    A rate is the current price for a currency pair, and it changes all the time. A quote locks that price for 30 seconds so you know exactly what you'll get.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Fees and settlement

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Are there conversion fees?">
    No separate fee. The rate in the quote is the all-in rate, and the quote's `fee` is always `0`.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How long does a conversion take?">
    Your source wallet is debited straight away, and the destination wallet is credited within 5 to 10 minutes. Fincra sends a `conversion.successful` webhook when it's done.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which currencies can I convert between?">
    See [Supported currency pairs](/fx-and-conversion/supported-currency-pairs). Most pairs work in both directions. CAD only converts to USD.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
