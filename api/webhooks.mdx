Fincra sends a webhook to your server when something happens in your account, such as a payout succeeding. Use webhooks to update your system instead of checking the status of every transaction.

## Turn on webhooks

Webhooks are off by default. Turn them on in each environment:

1. In the dashboard, open **My Account** > **API Keys & Webhooks**.
2. Enter your webhook URL and turn webhooks on.
3. Turn on **Auto-retry failed webhooks** so you don't miss events.

## Verify the signature

Every webhook includes a `signature` header. Check it before you trust the webhook, so you know it came from Fincra.

1. Take the `event` and `data` fields from the webhook body.
2. Create an HMAC SHA-512 hash of them as JSON, using your webhook key.
3. Compare the hex result with the `signature` header. If they don't match, ignore the webhook.

## Retries

With auto-retry on, Fincra retries a webhook your server didn't accept up to 5 times:

| Retry | Wait after the previous attempt |
|---|---|
| 1 | 5 minutes |
| 2 | 30 minutes |
| 3 | 2 hours |
| 4 | 6 hours |
| 5 | 24 hours |

Fincra waits up to 10 seconds for your server to respond. Respond with a `2xx` code quickly, then do any slow work afterwards.

## Handle duplicates

A retried webhook has the same event ID as the original. Record the IDs you've processed, and skip any you've seen before.
