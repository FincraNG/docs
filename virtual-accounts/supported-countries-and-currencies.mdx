Virtual accounts let your business and your customers receive money in the currencies below. Each account has its own bank details, so payers send a normal bank transfer and the funds go to your Fincra balance.

There are two types of account:

- **Local accounts** hold one currency and receive payments through that country's domestic payment system. For example, an NGN account receives transfers from any Nigerian bank over NIP.
- **Multi-currency accounts** receive USD, GBP, EUR or CAD.

The table shows the payment methods each account accepts.

| Country | Currency | Account type | Receive via local payment methods | Receive via SWIFT |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nigeria | NGN | Local | Bank transfer (NIP) | No |
| Ghana | GHS | Local | Bank transfer (GhIPSS) | No |
| Kenya | KES | Local | Bank transfer (PesaLink) | No |
| Tanzania | TZS | Local | Bank transfer (TIPS) | No |
| Canada | CAD | Foreign currency | Interac e-Transfer | No |
| (IBAN country) | EUR | Foreign currency | SEPA, SEPA Instant | (confirm) |
| United States | USD | Foreign currency | None | Yes (confirm) |
| United Kingdom | GBP | Foreign currency | (confirm) | (confirm) |
