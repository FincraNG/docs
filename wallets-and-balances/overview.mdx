Every Fincra business has a wallet for each currency it can use. A wallet holds your money in that currency: collections arrive in it, and payouts, conversions and fees are paid from it. Fincra creates your wallets when your business is created, so you never create one yourself.

## The four balances

Each wallet reports four balances. The ledger balance is the total, and it always equals the other three added together:

```text
ledgerBalance = availableBalance
              + lockedBalance
              + rollingReserveBalance
```

| Balance | What it is | Can you spend it? |
|---|---|---|
| `availableBalance` | Money you can use now | Yes. Payouts, conversions and fees come from here |
| `lockedBalance` | Money held while something completes: the amount of payouts still in progress, and incoming money Fincra has recorded but not yet released to you | No. It moves to available, or leaves the wallet, when the transaction completes |
| `rollingReserveBalance` | Part of your card payments that Fincra holds as a reserve and releases later | No. It is zero unless a reserve applies to your business |
| `ledgerBalance` | Everything in the wallet | No. Check `availableBalance` before you send money |

<Note>
Always check `availableBalance`, not `ledgerBalance`, before a payout or conversion. If your available balance can't cover it, Fincra rejects the request.
</Note>

## How a payout moves your balances

A payout changes your balances in two steps, so the money is never spent twice:

| When | Available | Locked | Ledger |
|---|---|---|---|
| You create the payout | Goes down by the amount | Goes up by the amount | No change |
| The payout succeeds | No change | Goes down by the amount | Goes down by the amount |
| The payout fails | Goes back up by the amount | Goes down by the amount | No change |

A failed payout returns the money to your available balance. Other debits that take time, such as a [BVN check](/api/verification/verify-a-bvn), follow the same steps. [List wallet transactions](/api/wallets-and-balances/list-wallet-transactions) shows each step as its own entry, with every balance before and after.

## How money comes in

- **Collections.** Money your customers pay into your [virtual accounts](/virtual-accounts/overview) or through [Payments](/payments/overview) arrives in the wallet of that currency. Fincra credits the amount minus its fee, and the statement shows both.
- **Card payments.** Card payments are recorded in your ledger and locked balances first, and Fincra moves them to your available balance when it settles them. If a rolling reserve applies to your business, part of each card payment goes to your rolling reserve balance instead.
- **Conversions.** A [conversion](/fx-and-conversion/overview) moves money from a wallet in one currency to a wallet in another.
- **Bank transfer top-ups.** In the dashboard, you can top up a wallet by bank transfer. Fincra gives you an account and a reference to pay with, you upload your proof of payment, and Fincra credits the wallet after it reviews the payment.

In sandbox, you can add test money to any wallet with one request. See [Test in sandbox](/wallets-and-balances/test-in-sandbox).

## How money goes out

- **Payouts** to bank accounts and mobile money wallets. See [Payouts](/payouts/overview).
- **Conversions** to a wallet in another currency.
- **Fees** for services you use, such as a BVN check.

## Know when a balance changes

Fincra doesn't send a webhook when a balance changes. To react to money moving, use the webhooks for the transaction itself, such as a payout or collection webhook. To check a balance, call [List wallets](/api/wallets-and-balances/list-wallets) with `currency`.

## Reconcile your wallets

Use [Get a wallet statement](/api/wallets-and-balances/get-a-wallet-statement) to match every movement of money against your own records for a period. See [Reconcile your wallets](/wallets-and-balances/reconcile).
