Move money between your Fincra wallets in different currencies, at a rate you see and lock before you convert.

## How it works

1. Get a quote for the currency pair and amount. It shows the rate and what you receive.
2. Create a conversion with the quote before it expires.
3. Fincra debits one wallet and credits the other.

A quote expires after a short time. If it does, get a new one.
