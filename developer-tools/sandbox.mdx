Sandbox is a separate environment for building and testing. Nothing you do in sandbox moves real money, and sandbox has its own secret key and Business ID. When your integration works end to end in sandbox, switch to your production key and base URL.

| | Sandbox | Production |
|---|---|---|
| Base URL | `https://sandboxapi.fincra.com` | `https://api.fincra.com` |
| Money | Test money only | Real money |
| Keys and Business ID | Your sandbox ones | Your production ones |

Every test value on this page only works with sandbox keys and `https://sandboxapi.fincra.com`. Production rejects them.

## Cards

Use these with Checkout or [Create a direct charge](/api/payments/direct-charges/create-a-direct-charge). Each card triggers a different authorisation step. Use the exact CVV and expiry shown: any other card fails with "Please use one of the test cards".

| Scenario | Card number | CVV | Expiry | What to enter | Result |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| No authorisation | `5319317801366660` | `000` | `12/33` | Nothing | Succeeds straight away |
| PIN | `5366139833864633` | `123` | `12/33` | PIN `1234` | Succeeds |
| PIN and OTP | `5333018248439266` | `123` | `12/33` | PIN `1234`, then OTP `123456` | Succeeds |
| Address check (AVS) | `4960215093757593` | `123` | `12/33` | Country `Nigeria` | Succeeds |
| Phone and OTP | `4960095193590452` | `123` | `12/33` | Phone `08034627201`, then OTP `123456` | Succeeds |
| Date of birth | `6500539739337524` | `000` | `12/33` | PIN `1234`, then date of birth `1998-01-01` | Succeeds |
| 3D Secure | `4084080000000409` | `231` | `12/33` | PIN `1234`, then OTP `123456` | Succeeds |
| 3D Secure (redirect) | `5433070000000009` | `231` | `12/33` | OTP `123456` on the bank page | Succeeds |
| Insufficient funds | `4084080000005408` | `000` | `12/33` | PIN `1234` | Fails: insufficient funds |

A wrong PIN, OTP, phone number, address or date of birth makes the charge fail with a message that says which one was wrong.

## Virtual accounts

In production, a collection only happens when your customer sends a transfer. In sandbox, you trigger it yourself with [Simulate a virtual account collection](/api/sandbox-simulation/simulate-a-virtual-account-collection). It sends the same webhooks you get in production.

| Amount | Result |
|---|---|
| Any amount | Succeeds |
| `999` | Fails |
| 10,000 or more in EUR or CAD | Held for review |

To open an NGN virtual account, use BVN `22222222222`.
