title: "Resolve Account"
description: "Check an account exists and get the name on it before you pay out."
---

Resolve an account to get the name on it before you send money. Compare that name with the one your customer gave you, and stop the payout if they don't match. A payout doesn't run this check for you.

## What you can resolve

You choose the account type with `type`, then send the details for that type. The currency tells Fincra which country's rails to check.

| Account type | `type` | Currencies | Identify it with |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bank account | `nuban` for NGN, `bank_account` for others | NGN, GHS, KES, ZAR | Account number and bank code. GHS uses the bank's SWIFT code. ZAR uses a PayShap ID |
| Mobile money | `mobile_money` | GHS, KES | Phone number and operator code |
| IBAN | `iban` | Any IBAN country | The IBAN |
| Interac | `interac_etransfer` | CAD | The email registered with Interac |

The response gives you the account holder's name. For Interac, it also tells you whether Autodeposit is on.

## How it works

1. **Collect the details.** Get the account number, bank or operator code, or IBAN from your customer.
2. **Resolve the account.** Call [Resolve Account](/api/verification/resolve-account) with the details.
3. **Compare the name.** If it matches, continue. If not, ask your customer to check their details before any money moves.

## When to use it

- **Before a payout**, so money doesn't go to the wrong account.
- **When someone adds a bank account**, such as a vendor or customer saving payout details.
- **When saving a beneficiary**, because Fincra saves beneficiaries exactly as you send them.

## Test it

Use the test accounts on the [Sandbox](/developer-tools/sandbox#resolve-account) page. Each one resolves to a known name or fails on purpose.
