Every request and response uses the same formats for common values.

## Amounts

Send amounts in the currency's major unit, as a number. For example, NGN 50,000 is `50000` and USD 25.50 is `25.5`.

These currencies have no decimal places, so their amounts must be whole numbers:

| Currency | Name |
|---|---|
| `RWF` | Rwandan franc |
| `UGX` | Ugandan shilling |
| `XAF` | Central African CFA franc |
| `XOF` | West African CFA franc |

Other currencies accept up to 2 decimal places.

## Currencies

Use three-letter ISO 4217 currency codes in capital letters, such as `NGN`, `KES` or `USD`.

## Countries

Use two-letter ISO 3166-1 country codes in capital letters, such as `NG`, `KE` or `GH`.

## Dates and times

Fincra returns dates and times in ISO 8601 format, in UTC, such as `2026-10-09T10:00:00.000Z`.

## Business ID

Your Business ID identifies your business. Some endpoints need it in the request body, in a field called `business`. Each endpoint page says when it's required.

Find your Business ID in the dashboard under **My Account** > **My Profile**. Sandbox and production have different Business IDs.
