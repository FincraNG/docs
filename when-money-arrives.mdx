Whichever way a customer pays you, through a [virtual account](/virtual-accounts/overview), a checkout, a card or mobile money, Fincra records the money as a **collection**. Every collection follows the same steps and sends the same webhooks, so you handle incoming money one way.

## What happens

1. Fincra receives the money and creates a collection, with its own `reference`.
2. Fincra checks it. Most collections pass straight away. Some need a review first. See [When Fincra reviews a collection](#when-fincra-reviews-a-collection).
3. Fincra credits your wallet in that currency with the amount minus its fee, and sends the `collection.successful` webhook.

If the payment doesn't go through, the status is `failed`, Fincra credits nothing and sends `collection.failed`.

## Webhook events

| Event | When Fincra sends it | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| `collection.successful` | Fincra credited your wallet | Give your customer what they paid for |
| `collection.additional-info-requested` | Fincra needs answers before it can credit the money | [Answer the questions](#when-fincra-reviews-a-collection) |
| `collection.failed` | The payment failed or Fincra declined it after review | Don't fulfil the order. Nothing was credited |

Each webhook's `data` includes the collection's `reference`. Use it with [Get a collection by reference](/api/collections/get-a-collection-by-reference) to confirm the status before you act.

## Fees and amounts

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `sourceAmount` | What the customer sent |
| `sourceFee` | Fincra's fee |
| `destinationAmountSettled` | What Fincra credits to your wallet: the amount minus the fee |

For example, a transfer of EUR 200 with a fee of EUR 10 credits EUR 190. Card payments go to your wallet's locked balance first, and Fincra moves them to your available balance when it settles them. See [Wallets and Balances](/wallets-and-balances/overview).

## When Fincra reviews a collection

Fincra reviews some collections before it credits them, for example large payments in some currencies. The collection's status is `pending`, and Fincra sends `collection.additional-info-requested`.

1. Call [Get requested information](/api/collections/get-requested-information) with the collection's `id` to see Fincra's questions, such as "Source of the funds".
2. Answer each one with [Respond to requested information](/api/collections/respond-to-requested-information). Send the question's `id` as `additionalInfoId`, and your answer as `text`, `urls` or both. You can answer each question once.
3. When you've answered them all, the collection's `pendingAdditionalInfoCount` is `0`. Fincra reviews your answers, then credits the money and sends `collection.successful`, or declines it and sends `collection.failed`.

## Find a collection

| To find | Use |
|---|---|
| All money you received | [List collections](/api/collections/list-collections) |
| Every transfer into one virtual account | [List collections](/api/collections/list-collections) with `virtualAccount` |
| The collection from a webhook | [Get a collection by reference](/api/collections/get-a-collection-by-reference) |
| A checkout payment, by your reference | [Get a collection by your reference](/api/collections/get-a-collection-by-your-reference) |

To reconcile collections with your wallet, use the `reference`: it matches your [wallet transactions](/wallets-and-balances/reconcile).

## Test in sandbox

Use [Simulate a virtual account collection](/api/sandbox-simulation/simulate-a-virtual-account-collection) to make each outcome happen:

- Any normal amount: `collection.successful`.
- An amount of `999`: `collection.failed`.
- 10,000 or more in EUR or CAD: the collection is held for review and Fincra sends `collection.additional-info-requested`, so you can test answering questions.
