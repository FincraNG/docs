A virtual account is a real account number you give to someone who pays you. When they send a bank transfer to it, Fincra credits your wallet in that currency and tells you by webhook.

## How it works

1. **Request an account.** Call the API with the account holder's details. Fincra issues the account number straight away for NGN, and after a review for other currencies.
2. **Share the details.** Give the account number and bank name to your customer, on your app, an invoice or a checkout page.
3. **Your customer pays.** They send a normal bank transfer from any bank in that country.
4. **Fincra credits your wallet.** The money lands in your wallet in the same currency, minus Fincra's fee.
5. **You get a webhook.** Fincra sends a collection webhook with the amount, the payer and your reference, so you can update the order or customer balance.

- Check where you can open accounts on [Supported countries and currencies](/virtual-accounts/supported-countries-and-currencies).
