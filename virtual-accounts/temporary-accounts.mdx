---
title: "Temporary accounts"
description: "Create a single-use account for one payment, then let it expire."
---

A temporary account collects one payment and then expires. Create one for each order or invoice, show it to your customer and Fincra matches the transfer to that payment for you.

## When to use one

- **Online checkout**: show a fresh account number at the payment step.
- **Invoices**: put a unique account on each invoice so payments reconcile themselves.
- **One-off payments**: collect a fee or deposit without opening a permanent account.

## How it works

1. Call [Create a temporary virtual account](/api/virtual-accounts/create-a-temporary-virtual-account) with the amount, your `merchantReference` and when the account expires.
2. Show the account number, bank name, amount and expiry time to your customer.
3. Your customer sends the exact amount before the account expires.
4. Fincra credits your wallet and sends you a collection webhook.

Temporary accounts are available in NGN.

<Warning>
After an account expires, it no longer accepts payments. Tell your customer the expiry time clearly.
</Warning>
