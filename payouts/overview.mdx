Send money from your Fincra wallet to bank accounts and mobile money wallets, in the same currency or across currencies.

## How it works

1. Fund the wallet you're paying from.
2. For a payout in another currency, get a quote to lock the rate.
3. Create the payout with the recipient's details and your own `customerReference`.
4. Fincra sends the money and tells you the result by webhook.

## Good to know

- **Beneficiaries**: save the people and businesses you pay often, so you don't collect their details every time.
- **Safe retries**: Fincra accepts each `customerReference` once, so you can retry a request without paying twice.
- **Verify first**: check an account number and name before you send money.
