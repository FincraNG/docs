Fincra supports currency conversion across a range of African and international currency pairs. Not all combinations are available, the tables below show which source currencies can be converted to which destination currencies.

<Note>
  Supported currency pairs are being expanded over time. If you need a pair that
  isn't listed here, contact your account manager to discuss availability.
</Note>

## Good to know

- **Real-time rates**, Exchange rates update continuously. Use the [Rates](/fx-and-conversion/rates) endpoint to check the current rate for any supported pair.
- **No conversion fees**, There are no separate fees for converting between currencies. The exchange rate you see in your quote is the all-in rate.
- **Quote validity**, Once you generate a quote, the rate is locked for **30 seconds**.
- **CAD is one-way only**, You can convert CAD to USD, but no other source currency can convert to CAD.
- **Not all payment currencies are convertible**, Fincra supports 17 currencies for payments, but not all are available for direct conversion. See [Supported Currencies](/payments/supported-currencies) for the full list of payment currencies.
