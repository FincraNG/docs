A virtual account is a real account number you give to someone who pays you. When they send a bank transfer to it, Fincra credits your wallet in that currency and sends you a webhook.

## Account types

- **Permanent**: keeps the same details for as long as you use it. Give one to each customer so you know who paid.
- **Temporary**: expires after the time you set. Use it to collect one payment, such as an order or invoice. Available in NGN.

## How it works

1. Request a virtual account through the API.
2. Share its details with the person paying you.
3. They send a bank transfer.
4. Fincra credits your wallet and sends you a collection webhook.
