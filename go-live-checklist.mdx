<hr />

Work through these steps before you send real money. Production has its own Business ID, secret key, public key and webhook key, so nothing from sandbox carries over.

## 1. Get your business approved

<Steps>
  <Step title="Complete business onboarding">
    In the dashboard, open **Onboarding** and complete each section: business information, documents, directors and shareholders. You need your incorporation certificate, articles of association and a utility bill for your business address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for approval">
    Fincra's compliance team reviews your submission and emails you when your business is approved, or if they need more information. You can keep building in sandbox while you wait.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 2. Get your production keys

<Steps>
  <Step title="Switch to live mode">
    Turn off the **Test Mode** toggle at the top of the dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate your keys">
    Open the profile menu, then **My Account** > **API Keys & Webhooks** > **Generate API Keys**. Enter the 6-digit code from your email, then copy your secret key, public key and webhook key.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Store them securely">
    Keep your secret key on your server, in an environment variable or secrets manager. Fincra shows it only once.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Never put your secret key in a browser, mobile app or public repository. If it leaks, generate a new key straight away.
</Warning>

## 3. Update your integration

- Change the base URL from `https://sandboxapi.fincra.com` to `https://api.fincra.com`.
- Replace your sandbox secret key, public key and webhook key with the production ones.
- Replace your sandbox Business ID with your production Business ID. [Get business details](/api/business/get-business-details) returns it.
- Search your code and config for any sandbox URLs or keys you missed.

## 4. Set up production webhooks

- In live mode, add your production webhook URL. Use your real server, not localhost or a testing tool. See [Configure webhooks](/developer-tools/webhooks/configure).
- Verify the signature on every event with your production webhook key. See [Verify webhooks](/developer-tools/webhooks/verify).
- Return HTTP `200` quickly and handle the same event arriving more than once.

## 5. Allowlist your server IPs

For payouts, add the IP addresses of the servers that call Fincra. You can add up to three in the dashboard. See [IP whitelisting](/developer-tools/ip-whitelisting).

## 6. Activate the products you need

Some products are ready once your business is approved. Others need your account manager to turn them on after a separate review.

| Product | Ready after approval | Needs activation |
|---|---|---|
| Checkout and payment links | Yes | No |
| Virtual Accounts | Yes | No |
| Payouts | Yes | No |
| FX and Conversion | Yes | No |
| Direct charge | No | Yes |
| Card and mobile money collections | No | Yes |
| Direct Debit | No | Yes |
| Issuing | No | Yes |

## 7. Run a live test

<Steps>
  <Step title="Send a small real transaction">
    Make a low-value payment, payout or conversion with your own details, depending on what you built.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the webhook">
    Confirm your server received the event, processed it and returned `200`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reconcile">
    Match the transaction in your records with the dashboard, using Fincra's `reference`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Summary

| Area | Check |
|---|---|
| Account | Business onboarding approved |
| Keys | Production secret key, public key and webhook key in place |
| Integration | Base URL is `https://api.fincra.com` and production Business ID is in use |
| Webhooks | Production URL added and signatures verified |
| Security | Server IPs allowlisted and secret key kept server-side |
| Products | Products you need are activated |
| Testing | Live transaction, webhook and reconciliation confirmed |
