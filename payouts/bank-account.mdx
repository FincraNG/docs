Send money from your Fincra wallet to a bank account. Set `paymentDestination` to `bank_account`.

## Supported countries and currencies

| Country | Currency | Rails |
|---|---|---|
| Nigeria | NGN | NIP |
| Ghana | GHS | Local bank transfer |
| Kenya | KES | Local bank transfer |
| Uganda | UGX | Local bank transfer |
| Zambia | ZMW | Local bank transfer |
| Tanzania | TZS | Local bank transfer |
| South Africa | ZAR | Local bank transfer |
| Egypt | EGP | Local bank transfer (account number or IBAN) |
| United States | USD | ACH, Fedwire, SWIFT |
| United Kingdom | GBP | FPS, CHAPS, SWIFT |
| Eurozone | EUR | SEPA, SEPA Instant, SWIFT |

## What you need

- The beneficiary's name, account number and bank code.
- For international payouts, the details the rail asks for, such as a sort code, routing number, IBAN or SWIFT code. See the country page in [Coverage](/payouts/payout-coverage).

## Send a bank payout

Follow [Initiate a transfer](/payouts/initiate-transfer) with `paymentDestination` set to `bank_account`. Fincra sends a webhook when the payout succeeds or fails.
