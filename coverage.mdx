## Africa

| Country | Currency | Payments | Payouts | Virtual Accounts | FX |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nigeria | NGN | Card, Bank transfer, Digital wallets (PalmPay, OPay) | Bank | Local (NIP) | Yes |
| Ghana | GHS | Mobile money, Virtual account | Bank, Mobile money | Local (GhIPSS) | Yes |
| Kenya | KES | Mobile money, Virtual account | Bank, Mobile money | Local (PesaLink) | Yes |
| Tanzania | TZS | Mobile money, Virtual account | Bank, Mobile money | Local (TIPS) | Yes |
| Uganda | UGX | Mobile money | Bank, Mobile money | No | Yes |
| Zambia | ZMW | Card, Mobile money | Bank, Mobile money | No | Yes |
| South Africa | ZAR | Card, EFT | Bank | No | Yes |
| Egypt | EGP | Card, Mobile money | Bank, Mobile money | No | No |
| Cameroon | XAF | Mobile money | Mobile money | No | Yes |
| Senegal | XOF | Mobile money | Mobile money | No | Yes |

## International

| Country | Currency | Payments | Payouts | Virtual Accounts | FX |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| United States | USD | Card | ACH, Fedwire, SWIFT | Foreign currency | Yes |
| United Kingdom | GBP | - | FPS, CHAPS, SWIFT | - | Yes |
| Eurozone | EUR | - | SEPA, SEPA Instant, SWIFT | SEPA, SEPA Instant | Yes |
| Canada | CAD | - | No | Interac e-Transfer | To USD only |

Direct Debit is available in Nigeria. Verification is available in Nigeria and Ghana.

Need a market that isn't listed here? [Contact support](mailto:support@fincra.com).
