---
title: "Direct Debit"
description: "Collect recurring payments from your customers' bank accounts, with their approval."
---

Collect payments straight from your customers' bank accounts on a schedule, such as subscriptions, loan repayments or instalments.

## How it works

1. Your customer approves a mandate that lets you debit their account.
2. You collect payments under that mandate, within the limits they agreed to.
3. Fincra credits your wallet and tells you the result of each debit.
