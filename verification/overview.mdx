Check that an account or identity is real before you send money or onboard someone. It cuts failed payouts and payments to the wrong person.

## What you can verify

- **Beneficiary accounts**: confirm a bank account or mobile money number and get the name on it.
- **BVN**: confirm a Nigerian Bank Verification Number belongs to the person who gave it.
- **Card BIN**: look up a card's brand, type and issuing country from its first digits.

In sandbox, use the test values on the [Sandbox](/developer-tools/sandbox) page.
