Sandbox is a separate environment for building and testing. Nothing you do in sandbox moves real money, and sandbox has its own secret key and Business ID. When your integration works end to end in sandbox, switch to your production key and base URL.

| | Sandbox | Production |
|---|---|---|
| Base URL | `https://sandboxapi.fincra.com` | `https://api.fincra.com` |
| Money | Test money only | Real money |
| Keys and Business ID | Your sandbox ones | Your production ones |

## Make things happen

In production, some events only happen when your customer acts. In sandbox, you trigger them yourself:

| To test | Use | Outcomes |
|---|---|---|
| A customer paying into a virtual account | [Simulate a virtual account collection](/api/sandbox-simulation/simulate-a-virtual-account-collection) | Any amount succeeds. `999` fails. 10,000 or more in EUR or CAD is held for review |

Each simulation sends the same webhooks you get in production.
